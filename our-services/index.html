Our services

We understand our clients' distinctive requirements and deliver cost-effective construction solutions to help them meet their business objectives.

01

Services

Building Construction

Ashok Constructions delivers excellent solutions in construction management and relieves clients' concerns by providing inputs on feasibility, budget, and timeline, managing and streamlining the project's complete scope, and collaborating with a network of skilled subcontractors.

We review every possible construction angle, troubleshoot risks, determine scenarios, estimate expenses, and determine costs before proceeding with the construction phase. This process enables us to provide our customers with a comprehensive view of the project and ensure we build the structure right the first time. We understand that costing is a critical factor for every project and our team has the mastery to provide clients with precise and competitive pricing and services.

  • Feasibility, budget and timeline
  • Complete scope, managed and streamlined
  • Network of skilled subcontractors
  • Precise and competitive pricing

02

Services

Road Construction

Ashok Constructions is one the leading road construction companies in Tamil Nadu, with professionals specially trained in road construction and advanced machinery and equipment capable of constructing tar, bituminous, paver, or asphalt / concrete roads.

Roadways improve connectivity between cities and growth centres thereby enhancing socio-economic development. We forge long-term relationships with our clients, workers, suppliers, contractors and stakeholders, which enabled us to sustain in a competitive environment.

We streamline each process and ensure our team remains productive with manageable tasks, increasing efficiency, minimizing costs, improving quality, and adhering to safety standards. Our competent project managers ensure that all stakeholders are clear on project expectations, thereby controlling the progress and ensuring quality and safety.

  • Tar, bituminous, paver, asphalt / concrete roads
  • Advanced machinery and equipment
  • Quality and safety standards
