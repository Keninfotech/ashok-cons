Our services

We understand our clients' distinctive requirements and deliver cost-effective construction solutions to help them meet their business objectives.

01

Sector Expertise

Building Construction

Ashok Construction delivers civil engineering excellence across commercial, educational, healthcare, hospitality, industrial, and government buildings throughout South India.

We recruit qualified and skilled professionals and utilize superior-quality construction materials; our strength lies in our expertise and efficacy to deliver projects within deadlines. We manage the complete scope—from structural engineering, deep foundations, and reinforcement through internal electrical, water supply, sanitary fittings, and finishes.

  • Commercial & Hospitality Buildings (Hotels, Bus Terminals, Commercial Complexes)
  • Healthcare Facilities (Multi-Speciality Hospitals & Govt Headquarters Hospitals)
  • Educational & Institutional Campuses
  • Industrial Buildings, Factories & Green Energy Solar Plants
  • Government Buildings, Court Complexes & Urban Habitat Tenements

02

Infrastructure

Highways & Road Formations

As a Class 1 Highways and DRDA contractor, Ashok Construction undertakes state highways, major bypass corridors, arterial ring roads, and complex hill formations.

Our capabilities cover sub-base stabilization, granular sub-base (GSB), wet mix macadam (WMM), dense bituminous macadam (DBM), and asphalt paving with modern sensor pavers. We execute multi-lane widening and strengthening with paved shoulders, difficult ghat road cutting, protective reinforced retaining walls, roadside drain networks, and geometric junction improvements designed for high-capacity traffic corridors.

  • State Highways & Bypass Formations (Namakkal Bypass Phase-I & Ring Road Phase-IV)
  • Ghat & Hill Road Formations (Bodhamalai New Formation Road, Kollihills)
  • Road Widening, Strengthening & Paved Shoulder Formation
  • Reinforced Earth Retaining Walls, Well Protective Walls & Drainage Works
  • Junction Improvements, Roundabouts, Pavement Markings & Road Safety Signage

03

Civil Engineering

Bridges & Cross-Drainage

Ashok Construction designs and constructs high-level river bridges, road overbridges, valley crossings, and heavy cross-drainage structures built to withstand extreme hydraulic forces.

We specialize in replacing vulnerable, submersible causeways with modern high-level bridges to guarantee uninterrupted, year-round connectivity. Our engineering scope spans deep well and bored pile foundations, reinforced concrete piers and abutments, pre-stressed concrete (PSC) and RCC girder fabrication, elastomeric bearings, high-durability deck slabs, approach road formations, guide bunds, and anti-scour river training works adhering strictly to IRC and MoRTH specifications.

  • High-Level River Bridges replacing Submersible Causeways (Ramanathapurampudur Bridge)
  • Ghat Stream & Valley Drainage Bridges (Kollihills High-Level Bridges)
  • Cast-in-Situ & Pre-cast Box Culverts, Slab Culverts & Minor CD Structures
  • Deep Substructure Engineering (Well Foundations, Pile Foundations & Heavy Piers)
  • River Training Works, Guide Bunds, Concrete Apron Protection & Approach Embankments

04

Public Utilities

Pipelines & Water Schemes

Class 1 registered contractor with the Tamil Nadu Water Supply and Drainage (TWAD) Board, delivering bulk water transmission mains, distribution networks, and civic utility pipeline infrastructure.

Ashok Construction undertakes comprehensive water distribution and public health engineering works under major government initiatives including AMRUT 2.0. Our execution covers pipeline route alignment, trench excavation through hard rock and soil, supply, lowering, laying, and jointing of Ductile Iron (DI), MS, HDPE, and UPVC pipelines, followed by hydrostatic pressure testing, construction of RCC overhead service reservoirs (OHT), sump and pump house stations, valve chambers, and household service connections.

  • TWAD & Town Panchayat Pipeline Projects (Mohanur AMRUT 2.0 Water Supply Scheme)
  • Potable Water Transmission Feeder Mains & Distribution Networks (DI, MS & HDPE)
  • RCC Overhead Service Reservoirs (OHT), Ground Level Sumps & Booster Pump Houses
  • Trenching, Rock Breaking, Thrust Block Anchoring & Hydrostatic Pressure Testing
  • Underground Drainage (UGD) Works, Culvert Crossing Mains & Sluice Valve Chambers
