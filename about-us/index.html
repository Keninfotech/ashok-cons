About Ashok Construction

Built on trust. Driven by progress.

Established in 2017, Ashok Construction undertakes building construction, highways, bridges and infrastructure projects across South India, with a focus on quality, efficiency and timely execution.

Headquarters / Operations Namakkal, Tamil Nadu

Company Profile

Ashok Construction is one of the leading construction companies in Namakkal, Tamil Nadu.

Since its inception in 2017, Ashok Construction has achieved a commendable track record of delivering a diverse range of projects for several leading organizations based in South India. We have steadily expanded our portfolio over the last nine years through our allegiance to building lasting relationships and surpassing clients' expectations.

We undertake commercial, education, healthcare, hospitality, industrial, and government buildings, State Highway projects, bridges, and TWAD & Town Panchayat pipeline projects. We recruit qualified and skilled professionals and utilize superior-quality construction materials; our strength lies in our expertise and efficacy to deliver projects within deadlines.

Our Roots

Our Journey of Diversification

From its beginnings in textile manufacturing in 1999, the Management has progressively diversified into infrastructure and construction, ready-mix concrete, agriculture, energy retail, education, and community development.

1999 • Manufacturing

Textile Mills & Green Energy

The Management traces its journey back to 1999 with the establishment of Ashok Textile Mills – Spinning Unit, expanding steadily into sizing and weaving. In 2017, the Made-ups Division was established for export-grade textile products, powered by a 100% Green Power Unit (Solar Plant & Windmill).

2017 • Infrastructure

Ashok Construction

Established as a dedicated division focused on public works, highways, and rural development projects, including projects undertaken for the National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) as well as private civil and industrial construction works.

Concrete Manufacturing

Ashok Ready Mix Concrete (RMC)

Manufactures and supplies high-quality ready-mix concrete to contractors, builders, and engineering professionals across Namakkal, Salem, and Trichy, supported by dedicated RMC infrastructure and logistics fleet.

Sustainable Agriculture

Ashok Natural Farms

Engaged in egg production for domestic and international markets, operating a dedicated native cattle conservation programme with over 200 native (desi) cows for milk and marketing natural products.

Energy Retail

Lakshmi Agencies

Operates as an authorised dealer of Indian Oil Corporation (IOC), serving commercial and consumer energy customers in the foothill region of Kollihills.

Education & CSR

Kongunadu Institutions & CSR

Partner of the management of Kongunadu Educational Institutions, Thottiyam, Tiruchirappalli (Engineering, Polytechnic, Education) serving ~6,000 students. Actively conducts tree plantations, lake and pond desilting, and school renovations.
